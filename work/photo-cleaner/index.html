Photo Cleaner

A local-first Flutter app for clearing out a photo library quickly, without ever deleting by accident.

Kado Digital LLCIn development2026
Discuss a similar project
◆

01 / THE IDEA

What it is

A photo-cleanup workflow built around quick decisions and deliberate control. Swipe right to keep, swipe left to queue for deletion, then review everything before deleting.

02 / THE BUILD

How it works

Flutter is the intended mobile stack. The design prioritizes authorized photo-library access, on-device analysis where practical, duplicate/similar-photo grouping and safeguards for sensitive images.

  • Swipe to keep or queue; deletion never happens without explicit confirmation
  • Local-first duplicate and similarity analysis, with attention to faces and sensitive documents
  • Optional monetization at natural breaks without interrupting or blocking progress

Tools and technologies

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • Mobile privacy
  • Local-first design

03 / STATUS

Where it stands

Prototype/development stage. The GitHub repository does not currently contain the complete app source, and no store release is claimed.
