01 / THE IDEA

What it is

Master’s-level investigation of how models can learn emotional cues in speech from time-dependent acoustic features and representations.

02 / THE BUILD

How it works

The research focused on Conformer-based modeling and pretrained speech representations, with experimentation and evaluation in an academic setting.

  • Research into spoken emotion classification and acoustic representations
  • Conformer architecture and pretrained audio representation experiments
  • Graduate research, not a public clinical or production system
