What can be tailored

Scope is set together, so you pay for what your site needs and nothing it doesn’t.

  • Brand and visual identity

    Your logo, colours and typography applied with care, plus art direction for imagery, motion and tone.

  • Layout and content

    Page structure planned around what your visitors need to find, from a focused single page to a multi-page site.

  • Integrations

    Contact and booking forms, maps, analytics, newsletters or other third-party tools you choose, connected where they’re useful.

  • Interaction and motion

    From quiet and editorial to cinematic scroll storytelling, always with reduced-motion and mobile-friendly fallbacks.

  • Deployment and domain

    Set up on reliable hosting suited to your business, connected to your domain, with search engine basics in place.

  • Ongoing care

    Optional support after launch for content updates, new pages and maintenance, agreed separately.

How a project runs

Five stages, each with a clear outcome you sign off before the next begins.

  1. Discovery

    We talk through your business, audience, goals and any sites you admire or dislike.

  2. Scope

    You receive a written proposal: pages, features, timeline and price. Work starts once the contract is agreed.

  3. Design

    Visual direction and key page designs, refined with your feedback.

  4. Development

    The site is built, tested on phones, tablets and desktops, and checked for accessibility and speed.

  5. Launch

    Deployment to your domain, a final review together, and a handover so you know how everything works.

Pricing

Every project is quoted individually and agreed in a written contract.

There are no fixed packages and no online checkout. The quote reflects the number of pages, the level of custom design and motion, any integrations, and whether you want ongoing care after launch.

Questions

Do you use templates?

No. Each site is designed for its owner. Proven, accessible building blocks are reused under the hood, but the look and structure are yours.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes, if you need to. Editing options are discussed during scoping so the setup matches how often you expect to make changes.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Every site is built responsive and tested on phones, tablets and desktops, with keyboard and screen-reader support in mind.

Who owns the finished site?

Ownership and licensing terms are set out in the contract before work starts, so there are no surprises.

Where will it be hosted?

On a hosting provider whose terms suit a business site. Options and any ongoing costs are explained in your proposal.
