A studio built by the person who writes the code.

Kado Digital LLC is an independent software studio. It makes its own apps and games, and builds professional websites for clients.

The studio

Kado started from a simple idea: build software that is genuinely pleasant to use, and take the time to get the details right.

The studio works on two tracks. The first is its own products, currently MemeLife, a mobile merge game, and Photo Cleaner, a local-first app for tidying a photo library. Both are in development. The second is client work: custom professional websites designed and built to a brief.

Kado is deliberately small. There is no layer of account managers between an idea and the code, so what’s agreed is what gets built.

What we care about

  • Worth using

    Every product and site should earn its place on someone’s screen. If we wouldn’t use it ourselves, it isn’t finished.

  • Craft in the details

    Motion, typography and interaction are part of the experience, never decoration that gets in the way of reading, tapping or finding things.

  • Accessible and fast

    Keyboard support, readable contrast, reduced-motion options and light pages are the baseline, not extras.

  • Honest about status

    We say plainly what’s released, what’s in development and what’s an experiment. No inflated claims.

Founder

Mobina Saffari

Mobina is a software engineer working in AWS cloud engineering and full-stack development. She holds a Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering.

She founded Kado Digital LLC independently and is its owner and developer: she designs, builds and ships the studio’s products and client websites herself. Outside the studio she explores AI tooling, including an experimental multi-agent coding assistant for VS Code.

Mobina’s employment at YAS Networks is separate from Kado Digital. YAS Networks is not a client, partner or affiliate of Kado, and work done there is not Kado’s property.
